# Historical structured data notice

The 88 source-labelled added profiles contain structured Wikidata main-namespace facts, identifiers and statement/reference metadata, available under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Policy: https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing . Each card records entity URL, exact revision and retrieval date (2026-11-08).

These are identity-reviewed, structured-source-checked cards, not 88 independently primary-source-verified biographies. Referenced statements can still be disputed. Conflicting, imprecise or non-Gregorian life dates are left unknown. Empty views/style/party fields are intentional. Office claims with incomplete day bounds are retained separately and do not establish availability or signing authority. Languages and affiliations are sourced claims, not inferred national stereotypes.

The five original primary-curated cards retain their original sources. No Wikipedia prose, portraits, game assets, software dependencies or third-party program code were imported. Exact game-localisation name matches establish a definition mapping only, not current office or ideology. Game data is read locally and not redistributed by this catalog.

Research caches under runtime/tmp are not release inputs. The application needs only people.json; the developer rebuild tool requires the explicitly reviewed cache and does not download data. New diplomatic text is original editorial material, fictional dialogue rather than historical quotations. Automated text checks do not certify native-language grammar.

Python, Ollama, Qwen and Microsoft WebView2 are NOT bundled binaries.
Their official installers/model downloads require separate explicit consent.
Installer URLs, licenses, sizes and SHA-256 are in the embedded installation plan.
Qwen must match release/model_profile.json; existing different models are not
silently replaced. Vendor runtimes retain their own terms, including bundled
GPU notices. Shared runtimes, models, user data and game saves are not removed.
